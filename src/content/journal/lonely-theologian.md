We can drink and indulge in the wisdom of Gods, but what does that mean for our condition?

We can merge with our souls and partake in the breads, but what does that mean for our mission?

Within, we see both sides of the journey, but what does that mean to the vision?

Between every pair is an infinite spectrum, where fusion is stronger than fission.

We wander the scrolls of an old haunted house with love for the cold apparitions.

The tinkerers’ booth at the end of this hall is the book which ignites all creation.

Every tinker must master which halls they journey before taking station.

Thus, the source of creation is both the beginning and end beyond all correlation.
